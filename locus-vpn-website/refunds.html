The {{policy.refundDays}}-day window

If you are not satisfied, ask for a refund within {{policy.refundDays}} days of your first payment and we will return it in full. You do not have to explain why. This applies once per account, to the first paid subscription on that account.

Annual plans

After the first 30 days, an annual plan is refunded pro-rata for each complete unused month remaining in the term. If you cancel 5 months into a 12-month term, you receive a refund for the 7 months you have not used. Partial months are not refunded.

Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions are not refunded after the {{policy.refundDays}}-day window. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Changing tier

Moving between Basic, Stream and Gaming takes effect immediately and is charged pro-rata for the remainder of the period. Downgrading is treated the same as upgrading — the difference is credited rather than charged, and there is no fee for moving down.

Extended outages

If the service is unavailable to you for 72 consecutive hours or more, and the cause was on our side rather than your network or device, you can claim a pro-rata credit or a refund for the affected period. Report outages through support so we can confirm the window.

How to request

  1. Open the dashboard and choose Billing → Request refund. Cancellation is on the same screen.
  2. Or email billing@locus.example from your account address.
  3. We reply within two working days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Card refunds typically appear within 5–10 working days, depending on your bank. We do not charge an administration fee for a refund.

Where we may decline

  • Accounts suspended for breaching the acceptable use policy.
  • A second refund request in the same {{policy.refundDays}}-day window, for a later subscription on the same account.
  • Requests made more than 12 months after the payment in question.

Your statutory rights

This policy sits alongside your rights under EU consumer law, and does not replace them. If you are in the EEA or UK and you have not used the service, you may withdraw within 14 days of purchase. Because the service begins immediately when you connect, we ask you to acknowledge that the withdrawal right ends once you first use it. The {{policy.refundDays}}-day policy above is more generous, so it applies in practice.
