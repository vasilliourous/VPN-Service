How it works

A tunnel at the network layer, not a proxy your apps can ignore

The client creates a TUN interface and captures traffic below your applications. There is no SOCKS5 proxy in the path, so no app can quietly bypass the tunnel by ignoring a proxy setting.

The engine runs as a supervised child process with automatic restart and fault reporting, and the server applies your tier's cap after decryption — which is what makes it hold on every device you own.

Read the architecture See the client

1 · Create an account

Confirm your email and you receive a subscription code, which is how the client knows the account is yours.

2 · Install and activate

Enter the code once. The first activation binds it to that machine's hardware fingerprint, which is what enforces the device limit rather than an honour system.

3 · Connect

The client picks the fastest reachable relay in your chosen country, brings the tunnel up, and shows you the route and measured latency while it runs.
