What you are agreeing to

Four questions we get asked before every purchase, answered without the hedging.

Can I change tier later?

Yes, in either direction and at any time. The difference is charged or credited pro-rata for the rest of your period. There is no fee for moving down a tier.

What if a payment fails?

Your connection keeps working for {{policy.graceDays}} days and the client shows the days remaining, so a lapsed card does not mean losing connectivity mid-task.

Is the refund real?

Full refund within {{policy.refundDays}} days of your first payment, no reason required, once per account. The refund policy has the detail.

Do prices change at renewal?

No. If we ever raise the price of your plan, you get at least 30 days' notice by email before it applies, and you can leave without penalty.

Start with any tier, leave whenever

Every plan carries the {{policy.refundDays}}-day refund and the same no-logs policy. Nothing here is designed to be hard to get out of.
