The short version

We keep an email address, a password hash and a billing record. We do not keep your IP address, the sites you visit, your DNS queries, or connection timestamps. There is no third-party analytics or advertising code on this site or in the client apps.

What we store

If you create an account, we hold the following:

  • Email address — your account identifier and the only way we contact you.
  • Password hash — generated with Argon2id. We never see or store the password itself.
  • Billing record — plan, signup date, renewal date and the country your payment method is registered in. Card numbers are held by the payment processor, not by us.
  • Device keys — the public half of each key the client generates. Private keys never leave your device.
  • Support correspondence — the contents of any message you send us, and our reply.

That list is exhaustive for the account system. If a category is not on it, we do not collect it.

What we do not store

  • The IP address you connect from
  • Your original IP address or the exit IP assigned to you, once a session ends
  • DNS queries you make while connected
  • Destinations, hostnames or URLs you visit
  • Connection timestamps, session lengths or bandwidth totals
  • Metadata about the packets passing through a server

Servers run with memory-only configuration, so there is no persistent session state to seize. Our infrastructure holds no disks in the data path.

How long we keep it

Account data is retained for as long as the account is open, then deleted within 30 days of closure. Billing records are kept for seven years, because tax law requires it. Support correspondence is deleted two years after the conversation ends.

Deletion means deletion. There is no dormant backup containing a copy of your account after the retention window closes.

Who we share it with

We do not sell data, and we do not share it for advertising. It is disclosed only to:

  • Payment processors — to take payment and handle refunds.
  • Email delivery providers — to send confirmation and receipt messages.
  • Authorities — where legally compelled. Because no traffic logs exist, in most cases there is nothing to produce. We report the number of requests received in our transparency report.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, export or erase your account data. You can export or delete everything from the dashboard without contacting us. For anything else, write to the address below.

Our data protection contact handles requests within 30 days. If you believe we have handled a request badly, you may complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens), our supervisory authority.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a data request: privacy@locus.example. For help with the service itself, see the FAQ.
