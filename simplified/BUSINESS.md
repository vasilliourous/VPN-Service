# Business Model & Operations

## The Core Insight

> Schools block free VPNs. Students want to game. Free VPNs are slow on congested WiFi.
> You provide a fast, reliable alternative that's hard to block — for cash, no credit card needed.

## The Three Tiers

### Eco ($2/month)
- Hysteria 2 with standard CC, artificially capped at 5 Mbps
- For: basic browsing, iMessage/WhatsApp, low-resolution YouTube (360p/480p)
- Small recv_window — feels sluggish, buffers often, annoying enough to want to upgrade
- Experience: "It works, just not great." Exactly the point.
- Why someone buys it: They're stingy. They want to pay the absolute minimum.
- **Sales role:** This is your decoy. It exists to make Stealth ($4) feel like a no-brainer upgrade.

### Stealth ($4/month)
- Hysteria 2 with standard CC, capped at 48 Mbps
- For: YouTube, Netflix, TikTok, Instagram, general browsing
- Large recv_window — smooth streaming, no buffering
- Experience: Fast streaming, but bufferbloat makes gaming unpleasant (by design for tier separation)
- Why someone buys it: They watch videos and want it to *actually work*.

### Strike ($8/month)
- Hysteria 2 with Brutal CC, bandwidth probe at connect, no artificial cap
- For: Minecraft, Fortnite, Roblox, Valorant, any online game
- Experience: Stable low latency, playable ping even on congested school WiFi
- Why someone buys it: They want to game during lunch and free periods.
- **Probe:** Downloads 500KB from VPS at connect, sets Brutal target to 80% of measured WiFi throughput.

## The Decoy Effect (Why Three Tiers)

```
                   Eco          Stealth        Strike
Price              $2            $4             $8
Speed              5 Mbps        48 Mbps        ~40 Mbps (adaptive)
Gaming capable?    No            No             Yes
Streaming?         Stutter       Smooth         Smooth
```

**Eco exists to sell Stealth.**

When Eco is an option, $4 for Stealth feels like a great deal — double the speed of Eco for only $2 more? Sold. Without Eco, some stingy users might argue "$4 is too much for a VPN." With Eco, $4 becomes the "reasonable middle option."

And Strike stands alone as the premium gaming tier — if you game, you need it. No substitute.

## Distribution: Middlemen

### How It Works

```
You (admin)          Middlemen (students)       End Users (students)
──────────           ──────────────────         ─────────────────────
Generate codes  →    Buy codes in bulk     →    Sell to friends
Run the server       Distribute to house        Collect cash
Handle tech          groups, year groups        Pay middleman
                     Take 20–30% commission     Get activation code
```

### Middlemen Operations

- **Who they are:** Students with social reach in their year/house. They're not technical.
- **How they get codes:** You generate batches in PocketBase admin. They send you cash/Bank transfer. You give them the codes.
- **What they sell:** Paper cards or a text message with the code. No app access, no admin panel.
- **Commission:** 20–30%. If Strike is $8, they keep $1.60–2.40 and pass $5.60–6.40 to you.
- **Why they participate:** Free money for minimal effort. Their friends need the service anyway.
- **Why middlemen love Eco:** At $2, Eco is an easy sell. "Try it for the price of a snack." Many will then upgrade to Stealth.

### Code Format

```
XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
```

Simple 12-character alphanumeric (uppercase, no confusing chars like O/0/I/1). No Luhn checksum. Generated server-side in PocketBase.

### Code Lifecycle

```
Generated by admin (unused)
        ↓
Sold to middleman (still unused)
        ↓
Given to student → entered in app → marked "used" in DB
        ↓
Permanent device binding via fingerprint.
If student stops paying → admin marks "suspended" in DB.
Can reconnect after suspension is lifted (same code, same device).
```

## Market Context

### Who You're Competing With

- **Free VPNs (X-VPN, Psiphon, Lantern, Hotspot Shield):** Getting blocked by school IT. Unreliable on congested WiFi. But they're **free** — which is why Eco exists as a $2 stepping stone.
- **xVPN:** The current king, but its protocol is becoming detectable.
- **Doing nothing:** The default. Students just accept they can't game at school.

| Factor | Free VPNs | Eco ($2) |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Speed | Terrible on congested WiFi | Rock-solid 5 Mbps via Hysteria 2 |
| Reliability | Drops constantly | Dedicated VPS, no overselling |
| Blocked? | School IT actively blocks them | Fresh IP, hard to detect |
| Cost | Free (but unusable) | $2 (but usable) |

The argument: "Free VPNs don't even work at our school. For the price of one snack, this one actually works."

## Operational Considerations

### When School IT Blocks You

- **Change VPS IP** — most blocklists are IP-based. Spin up a new droplet, update DNS, push new configs.
- **Change SNI** — use a different TLS SNI in your Hysteria 2 config (e.g., `www.bing.com` → `www.cloudflare.com`)
- **Change port** — 443 is standard, but 8443 or 2053 also work through most firewalls
- **Push new config** — update the `tier_configs` entries in PocketBase. Users get the new config on next connect.

### When Someone Stops Paying

Admin logs into PocketBase → finds the code → sets `suspended = true`. Next connection attempt gets `403`. App shows "Account suspended — contact your middleman."

### When You Need to Update the App

- Put the new binary on Google Drive or Discord
- Share the link in your group
- Windows: SmartScreen warning → "More info" → "Run anyway"
- macOS: `Ctrl+Click → Open` bypasses Gatekeeper

## Anti-Detection (Minimal)

For 20 users, the anti-detection strategy is:

1. **Don't use obvious VPN IPs** — Hetzner/DigitalOcean are fine. Avoid known VPN provider ASNs.
2. **TLS SNI disguise** — Set Hysteria 2's `sni` to a common website (`www.bing.com`, `www.cloudflare.com`)
3. **Obfs** — Enable Hysteria 2's salamander obfuscation
4. **Keep a low profile** — 20 users at ~2-5 Mbps each is invisible against a school's total bandwidth
